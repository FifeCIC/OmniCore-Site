## Support the Project

OmniCore is built and maintained by **LOLinDark** as a passion project for the
Star Citizen community.

### ⭐ Star the Repo

The simplest way to show support —
[star OmniCore on GitHub](https://github.com/FifeCIC/StarCitizen-OmniCore).

### 🐛 Report Bugs

Found something broken?
[Open an issue](https://github.com/FifeCIC/StarCitizen-OmniCore/issues) on GitHub.
The app's VerseMail form files issues for you if you are already in the app.

### 💬 Ask for Another Sim

Want OmniCore to support a different flight sim?
[Start a discussion](https://github.com/FifeCIC/StarCitizen-OmniCore/discussions)
naming the sim and your hardware.

### 🤝 Contribute

Pull requests are welcome. See the
[Developer Guide]({{ '/developer-guide/' | relative_url }}) for architecture and
contribution information.
